  <section class="block">
    <h2 class="block-title">About</h2>
    <div class="hero-bio">
      <p>Hi 👋&nbsp; I'm Jiyeon Bae, an M.S. student at <a href="https://www.snu.ac.kr/">Seoul National University</a>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://hcil.snu.ac.kr/people/jinwook-seo">Jinwook Seo</a> in the <a href="https://hcil.snu.ac.kr/">Human-Computer Interaction Lab</a>. I'm currently a Visiting Researcher in Prof. <a href="https://seas.harvard.edu/person/hanspeter-pfister">Hanspeter Pfister</a>&rsquo;s <a href="https://vcg.seas.harvard.edu/">Visual Computing Group</a> at <a href="https://www.harvard.edu/">Harvard University</a>.</p>
      <p>My research in human&ndash;computer interaction (HCI) explores how interactive systems can help people make sense of complex information, their own experiences, and AI behavior, and act on that understanding. Drawing on <strong>data visualization</strong> and <strong>human&ndash;AI interaction</strong>, I design and evaluate systems that support <strong>sensemaking and user agency</strong> by revealing patterns people might otherwise miss, enabling reflection, and helping people shape system behavior. I am particularly interested in how visual and interactive representations shape what people notice and understand. Looking ahead, I hope to extend these ideas to AI-assisted tools for visual authoring and creative exploration.</p>
    </div>
