CHERRY × Yanolja: Designing Accessible Donation Experiences

UX Research · Interaction Design · Industry–Academia Collaboration · Mar – Jun 2022

Overview

CHERRY × Yanolja is a UX design project exploring how charitable giving can be integrated into everyday travel and booking experiences. In collaboration with E4NET, our team proposed interaction designs connecting the CHERRY donation platform with Yanolja to make donating more accessible, convenient, and engaging.

User Research & Design Concepts

We conducted a survey with 40 participants to explore user behaviors and preferences, informing several proposed donation experiences.

  • Amenity-Based Donations: Designed an interface allowing hotel guests to opt out of unnecessary amenities, with matching donations tied to the items they decline.
  • Point-Based Giving: Proposed enabling users to donate unused or expiring Yanolja points through CHERRY, with reminders and simplified donation flows.
  • Participatory Donations: Explored donation experiences through DIY workshops and QR-based activities at travel destinations.

Outcome

Our proposal received first place in the industry–academia project evaluation by faculty and an industry representative, leading to an internship opportunity at E4NET.

My Contributions

As the leader of a four-person team, I contributed to user research, concept development, and UI/UX design, as well as the project proposal and presentation.
