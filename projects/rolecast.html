RoleCast: Role-Aware Multi-Agent Feedback for Broadcast Planning

Human–AI Interaction · Multi-Agent Systems · Apr – Jun 2026

Overview

RoleCast is a multi-agent system designed to provide early-stage feedback on broadcast production proposals. Grounded in real-world production data, it brings together AI agents representing different professional roles to offer specialized critiques, mediate conflicting perspectives, and generate actionable feedback before actual production meetings.

Key Features

  • Role-Aware Feedback: Four specialized agents representing producers, writers, editors, and AI/CG specialists evaluate proposals from distinct professional perspectives.
  • Grounded Multi-Agent Discussion: Agents use role-specific checklists derived from real production meetings to support evidence-based critiques and iterative conflict mediation.
  • Interactive Meeting Minutes: Generates structured summaries of key issues, decisions, unresolved conflicts, and revision suggestions, with support for follow-up questions.

Evaluation & Findings

We evaluated RoleCast against three baselines and two ablated variants across five broadcast proposals, followed by a user study with five industry professionals.

  • Automated Evaluation: RoleCast outperformed baselines on several metrics, including role specificity, actionability, and conflict resolution.
  • Expert Evaluation: Industry professionals rated a knowledge-grounded single-LLM baseline higher on most dimensions, although RoleCast showed advantages in decision-related feedback and grounding.
  • Key Insight: Stronger automated evaluation results did not necessarily translate into greater perceived usefulness, highlighting the importance of aligning AI-generated feedback with professional workflows and expectations.

These findings suggest opportunities to design multi-agent systems that balance diverse perspectives with practical decision-making needs.
