YouTrap: You’re Trapped in the YouTube Filter Bubble

Data Visualization · Human–AI Interaction · Oct – Dec 2025

Poster

Overview

YouTrap is a Chrome extension that helps YouTube users recognize and reflect on potential filter bubbles by visualizing their viewing habits and recommended content. It aims to make patterns of personalization visible and encourage awareness of how users’ information exposure may be shaped.

Key Features

  • Topic-Based Bubbles: Visualizes viewing patterns by topic, with bubble sizes representing watch counts or accumulated watch time.
  • Content Exploration: Lets users explore watched videos and related recommendations within each topic.
  • Long-Term History: Supports exploration of historical viewing patterns through Google Takeout, with data stored locally.

Evaluation & Findings

We conducted a pilot study and an exploratory user study, each with 12 participants.

  • Awareness Gap: 91.7% of pilot participants were unfamiliar with the concept of filter bubbles, although 83.3% reported experiencing them.
  • Self-Reflection: Participants recognized previously unnoticed viewing habits and reflected on the relationship between their consumption patterns and recommendations.
  • Viewing–Recommendation Relationship: The diversity of watched and recommended content showed a positive correlation (r = 0.72, p = .012).

These findings suggest that interactive visualization can support awareness and reflection, although its long-term effects on viewing behavior remain unexplored.
