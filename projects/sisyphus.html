Sisyphus: Interactive Media Installation

Interactive Installation · Projection Mapping · Physical Interaction · May – Jul 2022

Overview

Sisyphus is an interactive media installation inspired by the Greek myth of Sisyphus, exploring the repetitive nature of modern life and people’s ability to influence its pace. Combining physical sculptures, projection mapping, and responsive animations, the installation invites visitors to reflect on their agency within recurring routines.

Installation & Interaction

  • Viewer-Responsive Interaction: Uses camera-based tracking and Unity to adapt characters’ movements and animation speeds based on visitors’ positions and how long they remain in the space. As visitors approach, the characters work faster.
  • Physical–Digital Integration: Combines a sculpted mountain, rotating gears, and a motorized wheel with projected animations to represent repetitive labor and the passage of time.
  • 3D Animation & Projection Mapping: Integrates Maya animations with physical structures, connecting digital narratives with tangible objects.

Exhibition

Presented at Dot-To-Dot, Chung-Ang University’s graduation exhibition, reaching over 200 visitors. The installation received positive visitor feedback and attracted interest from a representative of the Korea Creative Content Agency.

My Contributions

As part of a three-person team, I contributed to concept development and interaction design, created 3D animations in Maya, and fabricated the motorized birch-plywood wheel.
